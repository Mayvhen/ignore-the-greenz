# Ignore the Greenz

Stop fighting the greenery. Ignore the Greenz makes your crosshair and your attacks look straight through decorative plants, so you hit the block or the mob that is actually behind them instead of the grass in front of it.

## What it ignores

- Leaves of every kind
- Short and tall grass, ferns, dry grass
- Bushes, firefly bushes, red shrubs
- Flowers – dandelions, poppies, tulips and the rest
- Vines, twisting and weeping vines, glow lichen, hanging roots
- Leaf litter, pink petals, wildflowers, moss and pale moss carpets
- Nether sprouts, crimson and warped roots, seagrass, shelf mushrooms

## What it deliberately leaves alone

Anything you might actually want to farm, harvest or interact with: crops and their stems, saplings and propagules, mushrooms, sweet berries, sugar cane, bamboo, dripleaves, cave vines (glow berries), spore blossoms, cactus, azaleas and lily pads.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 26.3
- Fabric Loader 0.19.5 or newer
- Fabric API 0.163.0+26.3

Built for the client: it works on any server and needs nothing installed server-side.
